How it works

From headlines to a report you can defend

Four steps, and every number traces back to a real article.

1

Collect

Pulls headlines from Malaysian news outlets. YouTube comments and Reddit can be connected when you are ready.

2

Tag

AI labels each item by sector, coalition, sentiment and stakeholder audience. You can check every tag against the original headline.

3

Measure

Set measurable objectives and report outputs, outcomes and impact for your organisation.

4

Act

Use the AI drafting tools for campaigns, crisis plans and stakeholder maps, then review before you use them.

Built for Malaysia

Local context, not a global template

Malaysian PR is shaped by coalitions, sectors and languages that generic tools were not built around.

  • Coalition-aware. Coverage is organised around BN, PH, PN, GPS and GRS, not a single left-right scale.
  • Sector-aware. Fifteen sectors, from banking and plantation to energy, telecom, property and tourism.
  • Reads English and Malay headlines. Tagging handles both. Accuracy is never perfect, so every tag sits next to its source.
  • Stakeholder audiences. Government, business, investors, consumers, civil society and workforce, as the first principle of good measurement asks.

Measurement you can defend

Aligned to the Barcelona Principles 4.0

AMEC's principles are the industry's reference for communications measurement. PRVAIL is built around them.

Objectives first. Set a measurable goal before you measure.
Stakeholder audiences. Know who you are trying to reach.
Quantitative and qualitative. Numbers sit beside the headlines behind them.
No AVEs. Advertising value equivalents are never used.
Outputs, outcomes, impact. Report all three, exportable as PDF.
Transparency. Sources, AI use and limits are disclosed on a methodology page.

Trust and transparency

Clear about what it knows, and what it doesn't

Every number has a source

Each article links to the original. Nothing is invented to fill a chart.

AI is labelled

Tags and drafts are marked as AI-assisted. Review before you rely on them.

Private workspaces

Each organisation's objectives and settings are kept separate behind sign-in.

Data minimised

No author names are stored from social sources, and social posts are deleted after 14 days.

Coverage today: online news from Malaysian outlets, plus YouTube comments and Reddit when connected. Facebook, TikTok, X, WhatsApp and broadcast are not covered, so results are not the whole conversation.
